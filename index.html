Account and Data Deletion

At Bewellery, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. You can request the deletion of your Bewellery account and associated personal data by following the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account:

  1. Send an email to YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL using the email address associated with your Bewellery account.
  2. Use Account Deletion Request as the email subject.
  3. State that you want your Bewellery account and associated personal data deleted. Include your registered email address.
  4. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing your request.

You do not need to remain signed in to the Bewellery app to request account deletion. You can submit your request by email.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your request is verified and processed, we will delete your account and associated personal data, where applicable, including:

  • Your account profile and personal details.
  • Your account credentials and authentication-related account data.
  • Personal information associated with your account that is not required to be retained.
  • Other associated personal data eligible for deletion under our data retention practices.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where a legitimate and lawful business need applies. This may include transaction or financial records, information needed to resolve disputes, or records required to prevent fraud.

Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary, protected appropriately, and kept only for the applicable retention period. Where required, we will explain the reason for retention and the relevant period.
